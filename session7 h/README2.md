Color Scheme: I chose a "Caffeinated Palette" using creams (#fdf5e6) and deep browns (#6f4e37). I chose this because it visually reinforces the theme of the content.

Margin vs. Padding: Padding is the space inside the element (between the text and the border), while Margin is the space outside the element (pushing other elements away).

Hardest Part: Getting the table to look modern instead of looking like a 1990s spreadsheet was the trickiest part.

Favorite Selector: tr:hover. It’s a simple line of code that makes the table feel interactive and professional.
