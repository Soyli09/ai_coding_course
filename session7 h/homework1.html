Whether you prefer a dark roast or a light, fruity blend, understanding the basics of brewing can transform your morning routine.

Popular Brewing Methods

There are dozens of ways to make coffee. Here are the most common ones found in home kitchens:

  • French Press: Heavy body and textured mouthfeel.
  • Pour Over: Clean, bright, and highlights flavor notes.
  • Espresso: Concentrated and intense.
  • Cold Brew: Low acidity and naturally sweet.

How to Brew a Simple Pour Over

  1. Boil water to roughly 200°F.
  2. Place filter in the dripper and rinse with hot water.
  3. Add 20g of medium-ground coffee.
  4. Pour water in circular motions and wait 3 minutes.
